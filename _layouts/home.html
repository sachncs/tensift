The 7-stage pipeline

  1. 1

    Lattice construction

    Build a Schnorr lattice that encodes the semiprime as a CVP instance.

  2. 2

    Basis reduction

    LLL, Segment LLL, or BKZ with pruning to improve the basis.

  3. 3

    CVP baseline

    Babai rounding and Klein sampling give a starting approximation.

  4. 4

    Tensor network ansatz

    Build a Tree Tensor Network over the residual Hamiltonian.

  5. 5

    Optimization & sampling

    TTN sweeps, OPES, and MPO spectral amplification find low-energy configs.

  6. 6

    Smoothness verification

    Trial division over the factor base, with sr-pair construction.

  7. 7

    Factor extraction

    GF(2) linear algebra + GCD turns smooth relations into the factors.
